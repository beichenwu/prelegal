Adaptations
-----------
The "*-cover.md" files are ADAPTATIONS, not unmodified copies. Common Paper
publishes each agreement's Cover Page / Order Form only as DOCX / Google Doc,
not as Markdown. These files were written for Prelegal as fill-in cover pages
in Markdown, following the structure and field set Common Paper documents for
each standard. They are derivative works under CC BY 4.0; changes were made.
The corresponding "*.md" Standard Terms they incorporate remain unmodified.
